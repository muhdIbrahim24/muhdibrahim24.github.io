# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineering hiring managers, technical reviewers, collaborators, and research or design teams evaluating Muhammad Ibrahim's mechanical, civil, simulation, research, fabrication, and technical-communication experience.

## Product Purpose

Present verified engineering work, methods, results, limitations, credentials, experience, research, activities, contact information, and UK work authorisation with enough clarity and visual distinction to support hiring or technical review.

## Brand Personality

Machined, nocturnal, calibrated.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS card grids; beige editorial portfolios; terminal cosplay; neon cyberpunk dashboards; identical project tiles; fabricated technical diagrams; decorative metrics; imagery that implies stock photography is project evidence.

## Design Principles

- Lead each chapter with one dominant engineering idea.
- Show evidence and limitations with equal confidence.
- Use imagery as atmosphere while labelling its provenance truthfully.
- Compress dense career history without hiding detail.
- Make motion directional, reversible, and optional.
- Keep every experience usable without external services or a build step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; full keyboard operation; visible focus; reduced-motion support; sufficient contrast over imagery; responsive reflow through 320px and 200% zoom; no hover-only information.
