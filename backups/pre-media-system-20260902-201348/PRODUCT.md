# Portfolio product context

## Register

Engineering portfolio for hiring teams, technical reviewers and collaborators. Platform: static web.

## Purpose

Make verified career evidence and its limitations easy to review without overstating project proof.

## Personality

Measured, image-led, assured.

## Principles

- Show evidence and its limitations together; verified copy is never inflated.
- Label every stock image as temporary and specify whether it is not project, research or activity evidence.
- Keep career history compact: two research records, a four-role experience timeline and six activities.
- Treat reversible motion as optional enhancement; every item remains readable without JavaScript.
- Keep static GitHub Pages deployment dependency-free at runtime.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS grids, terminal cosplay, fabricated metrics, unlabelled stock imagery and opaque dark interfaces that make career evidence hard to read.

## Quality bar

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access; self-hosted OFL fonts; one reusable dialog retains loading, future, error, GIF-poster, lightbox and focus-restoration behavior.
