The person behind the drawings.

Your photo
Caption: where the photo was taken, or what you were working on.

Open with one line about who you are and what you do. Then two or three sentences on your background, the kind of engineering you gravitate toward, and what you are looking for next. Write it the way you would say it out loud rather than the way a CV reads.

Use the second paragraph for the working style: whether you prefer hands-on experimental work, computational modelling, or design, and what you have actually spent the most hours doing. Being specific here is what separates this from every other portfolio page.

Close with what you want next. Since you are actively looking, say so plainly and say what kind of role, so that anyone reading knows exactly what to offer you.
