# Education-card background prompts (Google Gemini / "Nano Banana")

Three prompts, one per study record on the site's new Education strip. Same house style as the project-cover prompts: editorial photography, restrained neutral-to-warm palette with room for one muted accent colour, no visible text, logos, watermarks, UI overlays, faces or people, and a wide 3:2 crop. These sit behind white text on a dark purple-tinted scrim, so ask for a fairly simple, low-clutter composition — a busy image will fight the type once the scrim is applied.

Until real photographs are generated, each card uses a plain duotone treatment (the same purple grid pattern as the hero, repositioned per card) so the strip already looks intentional rather than empty.

---

## New York University Abu Dhabi

```
A wide documentary photograph of the New York University Abu Dhabi campus at golden hour: pale sandstone-and-glass academic buildings with a distinctive perforated screen façade, seen across a calm reflecting pool or waterfront promenade, palm trees along the edge. Warm low-angle desert light, long soft shadows, a clean modern-Gulf-architecture feel. No people, no text, no signage, no visible university crest or wordmark. Wide 3:2 composition, the façade filling the frame with the water in the foreground.
```

## New York University Paris

```
A quiet documentary photograph of a Parisian streetscape at dusk: pale Haussmann-style limestone façades with wrought-iron balconies, a row of warm streetlamps just switching on, a hint of the Seine or a bridge in the distance. Soft blue-hour light with warm window glow, gentle desaturation, editorial travel-photography style. No people in sharp focus, no text, no readable shopfront signage, no landmark close enough to be a cliché postcard shot. Wide 3:2 composition, the street leading the eye toward the middle distance.
```

## NYU June Term, Valletta, Malta

```
A wide documentary photograph of Valletta, Malta's honey-coloured limestone architecture at golden hour: tightly packed historic buildings with traditional enclosed wooden balconies, a narrow street sloping toward a glimpse of the Mediterranean at the far end. Warm late-afternoon light on the stone, soft shadows, a coastal old-city atmosphere. No people, no text, no shopfront signage. Wide 3:2 composition, the street's vanishing point toward the sea.
```

---

### Using these

If Gemini adds stray text or makes the scene too busy, add *"simplify the composition, remove any text or signage, keep it calm and uncluttered — this will sit behind white type."* Once you have images you like, send them over and I'll drop them in as each card's background photograph, cropped to the strip's proportions, with the current purple-grid treatment staying as a subtle base layer beneath.
