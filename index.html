  1. 01

    Marine systems / simulation 2024–25

    Advanced Anti-Roll Tank System

    A hydraulically controlled anti-roll tank for large vessels: port and starboard reservoirs whose water shifts out of phase with the ship’s roll, evaluated as a passive U-tube tank and then with an actively actuated loop.

    The passive tank cut roll amplitude by 85%; closing the loop with a hydraulic actuator took the reduction past 98%.

  2. 02

    Energy systems / CFD / prototyping 2024–25

    Savonius Turbine

    A vertical-axis turbine designed to harvest the vortices thrown off traffic moving in opposite directions either side of a central reservation. Optimised in ANSYS Fluent, then built and measured at 1:3 scale.

    Serrations raised peak CFD moment by 71.65% and power by 56.33%; the 1:3 prototype peaked at 5–7 V on the bench.

  3. 03

    Civil design / BIM coordination 2025

    5th Corner Residential Building

    A graded-A, four-storey residential building model and coordinated drawing package for a hot-arid Abu Dhabi context: architecture, structure, façade and schedules held in one Revit model.

    Column schedules and take-off automated from over 30 minutes to under one minute across all 84 columns.

  4. 04

    Personal project / UAV design 2026–present

    Search-and-Rescue UAV

    An assembled fixed-wing prototype with a detachable nose, intended to carry and release a smaller multirotor payload. Airframe, structure and materials owned on a two-person project.

    Airframe mass reduced from 2,500 g to 1,500 g (a 40% reduction) on a 1,130 mm wingspan.

  5. 05

    Thermal systems / instrumentation 2025

    TEC Refrigerator

    A compact compressor-less refrigerator built around thermoelectric (Peltier) modules driven in parallel, with Arduino sensing and closed-loop PWM control.

    Cabin cooled from a 27°C start to a stable 10°C across a 180-minute test, reaching equilibrium in about 120 minutes.

  6. 06

    Water systems / techno-economic analysis 2026

    SWRO Pretreatment Design, Arabian Gulf

    A conceptual pretreatment train for a 24,000 m³/day seawater reverse osmosis plant on the Sharjah coast, carried through a 30-year techno-economic lifecycle simulation with equipment-reliability and carbon-footprint modelling.

    A 30-year lifecycle simulation projects a cumulative pretreatment cost near 450 million USD, discounted at 6%, at a 0.52–0.78 USD/m³ unit cost.
